\## Real-World Incidents



\- \*\*Air Canada Chatbot Hallucination (2024)\*\* — Air Canada's 

&#x20; LLM chatbot fabricated a bereavement fare policy. Court 

&#x20; ruled Air Canada liable for its chatbot's hallucinated 

&#x20; advice, ordering compensation to the passenger.



\- \*\*DoNotPay AI Lawyer (2023)\*\* — AI legal service provided 

&#x20; confidently incorrect legal advice including fabricated 

&#x20; case citations. Regulators intervened over unauthorized 

&#x20; legal practice concerns.



\- \*\*Amazon Automated Pricing (2011, pre-LLM)\*\* — Automated 

&#x20; system without human oversight priced a textbook at 

&#x20; $23 million due to algorithmic feedback loop. Early 

&#x20; case study in automation overreliance risk.
